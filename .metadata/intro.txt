【原神】探索提瓦特
一个小型辅助向二次元mod。
[h1]本mod包含以下内容：[/h1]
[olist]
    [*]一个建筑【冒险家协会】。
    [*]一个国际组织【冒险家协会】。
    [*]截止《原神》月之三版本所有的原神角色。
    [*]一个阶层【「神之眼」持有者】以及阶层特权。原神角色从属于该阶层。
    [*]提瓦特文化。
[/olist]
[h2]使用方法[/h2]
[olist]
    [*]在首都建造建筑【冒险家协会】，过月后便会自动加入国际组织【冒险家协会】。
[/olist]

[Genshin] Discover Teyvat
A small auxiliary-style anime-themed mod.
[h1]This mod includes the following content:[/h1]
[olist]
    [*]A building: [Adventurers' Guild].
    [*]An international organization: [Adventurers' Guild].
    [*]All Genshin Characters up to NK.3.
    [*]An estate: [Vision Wielders] along with its privileges. Genshin Characters belong to this estate.
    [*]Teyvat culture.
[/olist]
[h2]How to Use[/h2]
[olist]
    [*]Construct the [Adventurers' Guild] building in your capital. After one month passes, you will automatically join the international organization [Adventurers' Guild].
[/olist]
